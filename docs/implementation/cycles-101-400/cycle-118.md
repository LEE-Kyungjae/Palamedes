# Improvement Cycle 118

## Topic

Expose whose imagined future a possibility encodes.

## Deficiency

Cycle 117 allows Palamedes to propose valuable possibilities beyond current
demand. Without stronger humility, the system could project its makers'
interests onto beneficiaries and label that projection a latent desire.

## Improvement

Added `validate_informed_possibility_exposure` and an experimental schema.

Every imagined future identifies its imaginers, their standpoints and
interests, encoded assumptions, and excluded perspectives. It must be shown
through a low-cost, low-risk, reversible artifact that creates no commitment
and gathers multiple situated-response signals to an informed preference
question.

## Scope boundary

Cycle 118 defines humble exposure. Cycle 119 must close the loop by updating a
desire hypothesis from the situated response rather than merely collecting it.

## Resulting invariant

Palamedes cannot present its own imagined future as beneficiary demand; it must
disclose authorship and invite cheap, informed correction.
