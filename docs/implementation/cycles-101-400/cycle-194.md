# Improvement Cycle 194

## Topic

Locate creativity in beneficiary discovery and mechanism transfer.

## Deficiency

Creativity could be measured as a novel product shape, rewarding superficial
variation without discovering a new external condition or a mechanism that
changes which missions are possible.

## Improvement

Added `validate_purpose_creativity_transfer` and an experimental schema.

A creative purpose record identifies a previously unexplicit, sourced
beneficiary condition and transfers a mechanism between distinct contexts. It
preserves the transfer invariant, target adaptation, opened action, obvious
product-form alternatives, constitutional trace, and at least two usefulness
tests. Product-form novelty alone is explicitly insufficient.

## Scope boundary

Cycle 194 defines the source of purpose creativity. Cycle 195 will require an
insight to create, remove, sequence, or reprioritize missions in the actual
mission landscape.

## Resulting invariant

Creative purpose formation changes the beneficiary condition or reachable
mechanism, not merely the appearance of the proposed product.
