# Improvement Cycle 379

## Topic

Bound the initial empirical quality-or-labor claim.

## Deficiency

A gain on one convenient metric can be inflated into a claim of general
superiority or startup success. Labor savings can also hide more constitutional
violations or proxy harm, while unequal information makes the comparison
meaningless.

## Improvement

Added `validate_initial_empirical_claim_boundary` and an experimental schema.

The claim recomputes direction-aware quality improvement and pre-planner labor
retirement under verified equal information. At least one must improve, while
both constitutional violations and registered proxy harms must be no worse
than baseline. The claim basis must match the measured gains. Startup success,
general superiority, and implications about unmeasured dimensions are
forbidden.

## Scope boundary

Cycle 379 bounds the first defensible empirical claim. Cycle 380 will integrate
mission consequence and cognition retirement with exposed information,
compute, labor, calibration, harm, and replaceability.
