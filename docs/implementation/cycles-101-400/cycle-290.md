# Improvement Cycle 290

## Topic

Integrate the end-to-end purpose proof thesis.

## Deficiency

Separate controls do not prove that autonomous purpose formation caused a
better real result. A convincing case must connect evolving signals to a chosen
mission, downstream action, measured outcome advantage, and reduced human
upstream labor without triggering a validity failure.

## Improvement

Added `validate_end_to_end_purpose_proof_thesis` and an experimental schema.

The proof links all Cycle 281–289 controls and requires a chronological
signal–interpretation–selection–planning–action–outcome lineage. It reconciles a
positive preregistered outcome advantage against the best baseline and a
positive measured reduction in human upstream labor. All proof guarantees and
independent verification must hold, with no triggered failure criterion.

## Scope boundary

Cycle 290 defines what a demonstrated single-case proof must contain; it does
not fabricate such empirical evidence. Cycle 291 will redefine the concrete
preplanning unit as a versioned mission selected under explicit values and
authority.
