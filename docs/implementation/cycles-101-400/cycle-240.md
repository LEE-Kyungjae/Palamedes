## Deficiency

system can still share one frame, produce internal activities, lock in a
mechanism, or rely on metaphor to carry apparent originality.

## Improvement

Added `validate_invention_thesis_integration` and an experimental schema.

The gate requires condition, capability, lineage, opposition, temporal, and
counterfactual transformations from unique isolated contexts before candidate
reveal. Every candidate links evidence and an external-condition contract.
Selection waits for independent generation, implementations remain open, and
skeptical translation must preserve the beneficiary change. Invention is
explicitly not a novelty label.
