# Improvement Cycle 119

## Topic

Update generative desire hypotheses from situated response.

## Deficiency

Cycle 118 creates a safe and humble exposure, but collecting responses alone
does not make discovery empirical. Palamedes could preserve its original
hypothesis regardless of what comprehending beneficiaries actually said or did.

## Improvement

Added `validate_situated_desire_update` and an experimental schema.

The update links possibility and exposure, preserves prior and revised
hypotheses and confidence, and records whether evidence strengthens, weakens,
revises, or retires the hypothesis. Responses must demonstrate comprehension,
retain their contexts and sources, and include a challenge or uncertainty.
Confidence movement must agree with strengthening, weakening, or retirement.

## Scope boundary

Cycle 119 closes the generative-empirical loop. Cycle 120 must integrate the
desire thesis into a mission candidate centered on human capability or
condition change rather than demand.

## Verification

- focused mission tests: 81 passed cumulatively
- `git diff --check`: passed

## Resulting invariant

An imagined possibility becomes a learned desire model only through explicit,
directionally consistent updates from situated and comprehending responses.
