# Improvement Cycle 193

## Topic

Govern autonomy through a living constitution, not approval theater.

## Deficiency

Delegation could remain a final human approval gate, preserving the very
upstream judgment dependency Palamedes is meant to retire while only adding
constitutional language around it.

## Improvement

Added `validate_living_constitution_runtime` and an experimental schema.

Routine decisions are interpreted against live, versioned values, uncertainty,
authority, representation, and correction state. Human approval is not the
default. Palamedes may act, probe, or defer within the constitution and
escalates only a named genuinely ungranted power.

## Scope boundary

Cycle 193 makes the constitution operational. Cycle 194 will locate creativity
in discovering new beneficiary conditions and transferring mechanisms rather
than producing novel product forms.
