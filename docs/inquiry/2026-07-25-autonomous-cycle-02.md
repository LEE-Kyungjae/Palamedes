## Independent cognitive pressure

collapsing:

1. mission artifacts receive higher blinded scores;
2. a mission changes a consequential choice;
3. delegated mission origination retires human judgment without moving that
   labor into setup, supervision, correction, or remediation.

rejected approaches that counted non-intervention, adoption, novelty, or owner

labor, expectancy bias, or inferior beneficiary outcomes.

The selector chose the live delegated-decision trial and rejected further
model-review accumulation as the next proof frontier.

## What must be measured

Before seeing the generated mission, preregister:

- one nontrivial but reversible project decision;
- frozen inputs and the exact authority boundary;
- which owner contributions would count as a decisive frame element;
- the normal-work counterfactual and material labor-reduction threshold;
- all owner setup, curation, monitoring, review, correction, and remediation
  time;
- beneficiary-outcome and correction-burden non-inferiority thresholds;
- complete interaction logging and the review window.

Success requires all of the following:

- no decisive frame element supplied by the owner before mission commitment;
- an attributable consequential downstream choice;
- total owner burden below the preregistered threshold;
- explicit owner attestation consistent with contemporaneous records;
- beneficiary outcomes and correction burden meeting the non-inferiority
  thresholds.

## Falsifiers

- The owner supplies a decisive frame element.
- Total burden is not materially lower after including experimental overhead.
- Attestation is absent or conflicts with interaction and time records.
- Beneficiary outcomes or correction burden miss their thresholds.
- No consequential choice is attributable to the mission.
- Audit finds hidden steering, displaced labor, selective instrumentation, or
  a material protocol deviation.
- The case was not genuinely consequential, reversible, or contested.
